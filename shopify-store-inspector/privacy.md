Shopify Store Inspector is a Manifest V3 Chrome extension published by Wade Barrie, operating as WB Digital in Vancouver, BC, Canada. This page is the privacy policy for that extension. It describes the extension only.

**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

## What the extension does

Shopify Store Inspector opens in the Chrome side panel. It analyzes the tab you currently have open, looks for Shopify storefront signals on that page, and shows the result in the panel:

- theme
- apps visible on the page
- page weight
- an SEO audit of the page

The extension does not require an account. There is no sign-in.

## The audit runs on your device

The analysis is done locally in your browser. The extension does not send browsing data or page contents to WB Digital, or to any other remote server, for the audit.

## What is not collected

WB Digital does not receive, store, or sell any of the following from the extension:

- your name, email address, or other contact details
- account or sign-in credentials
- payment information
- browsing history, or a list of the pages and stores you open
- the contents of pages you visit, including the theme, app, page-weight, and SEO details shown in the panel
- advertising identifiers, analytics events, or usage telemetry

The panel shows the audit to you. Those results are not uploaded to WB Digital.

## Preferences stored on your device

The extension may use `chrome.storage` only for local interface preferences, such as a display setting in the panel. Those preferences stay in your browser. They are not sent to WB Digital. Removing the extension removes them from that browser profile.

A version of the extension that does not save preferences does not write to `chrome.storage`.

## Permissions, in plain language

Chrome shows the extension's permissions when you install it. They are there so the side panel can audit the tab you are viewing:

- **Side panel.** Displays the theme, visible apps, page weight, and SEO audit next to the page you already have open.
- **The open tab.** Lets the extension read that page on your device and detect Shopify storefront signals. This access is used to show you the audit. It is not used to keep a record of the sites you visit, and the page is not sent to WB Digital.
- **Storage.** Used only for the local interface preferences above, and only when the extension saves a preference.

The extension does not ask for your Google account, contacts, or files.

## Other parties

WB Digital does not share extension data with anyone else. The extension does not send that data to us, so there is nothing from the audit for us to pass on.

You install the extension from the Chrome Web Store. Google's handling of your Chrome account and of the install is covered by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
